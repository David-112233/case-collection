Offline and browser replay of archived bytes were not tested; system-font fallback is defined in the own CSS. The unchanged author page may make external font and analytics requests on load.

Cloud browser, desktop: scroll to the diagram; toggle ANIMATE off while leaving LABELS on. Compare Distant object and Close object and then pause. Near focus has a rounder lens in the same front optical stack; white/brown/gold sclera, choroid and retina bands nest around the rear, ending at the optic nerve. At 50 light control, the site readout showed 5.0mm/+17 D with Distant and 4.2mm/+21 D with Close; these readouts are recorded as observed page states, not medical measurements. The author explicitly marks the model as stylized and not to scale. Did not submit the newsletter form or open third-party links.
